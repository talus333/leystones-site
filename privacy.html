Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026. Leystones is run by Jeremy Plante in Ontario, Canada ("we", "us").

Leystones is an app about places. To work, it needs to know where you are while you use it. This policy says what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to remove it. We do not sell your information and we do not show adverts.

What we collect

What Why Who can see it
Email address and password (the password is stored scrambled; we cannot read it) To sign you in Only you and us
Display name and handle So friends can find you and your stones carry a name Other people using the app
Stones you set: words, photos, place, who it is for This is the heart of the app Whoever you chose: everyone, friends, or one person
Layers you add to stones: words, photos, recordings Same Whoever can see that stone
Visits, finds, Waybadges, the parts of the map you have uncovered To unlock stones and keep your progress Only you
Journal, excursions and leylines To keep your record of where you went Only you, unless you share one
Friends, blocks, and reports you send To connect you, and to keep people safe Only you and us (the person you block or report is not told)
A notification token for your phone To send you notifications you have turned on Only us
A count of what you asked to identify or look up, with the top answer To keep daily limits fair Only you and us

We do not collect your contacts, your advertising identifier, or analytics about how you tap around the app.

Your location

  • While the app is open, your phone uses your position to draw the map, unlock stones you reach, and record the excursion you started.
  • In the background, only if you allow "Always" and only during an excursion you started, so the route keeps recording with the phone in your pocket. It stops when the excursion ends.
  • Your home zone stays on your phone. It is never sent to us. Finds inside it are not saved with a place at all.
  • Finds are saved rounded to about 100 metres.
  • Nearby lookups for map layers (wildlife, heritage, paddling, places to stop, protected land) send only a coarse square about 10 km across.
  • Search here and place lookups send the point you are searching around to public sources through our server, without your name or account attached.
  • Follow along shares your excursion with the friends you pick, for that excursion only.
  • Stones are saved at the exact place you set them. That is what makes them findable, so think before setting a public stone at your home.

Photos, camera and microphone

  • Photos on stones and layers are uploaded and stored so others can see them.
  • Naming a plant sends that one photo to Pl@ntNet. We do not keep it.
  • Naming an animal (when available) sends that one photo and a coarse 10 km square to iNaturalist. We do not keep it.
  • Reading a plaque or sign happens on your phone. The photo is not sent anywhere.
  • Naming a bird or animal by sound happens on your phone. The recording is not sent anywhere unless you choose to add it to a stone.

These services have their own privacy policies. We send them the least we can, and requests go through our server where possible so they do not see who you are.

When we share information

We share your information only in these cases:

  • With the people you chose, when you set a stone, add a layer, share a journal or let friends follow along.
  • With the outside services above, to do the one job listed.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. Reports are kept as long as needed to deal with them. Short-lived caches of public facts about places hold no personal information.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Me page, under "Delete my account". You choose one of two things:

  • Delete everything. Your account, stones, layers, photos, recordings, journal, badges, friends and visits are removed.
  • Keep my public stones, without my name. Everything is removed except the stones you set for everyone. Those stay on the map with their words and photos and no name. They no longer point to you, and you will not be able to change or remove them afterwards.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.

Your choices

  • Turn location, camera, photos, microphone or notifications off in your phone's settings. Parts of the app will stop working without location.
  • Change your display name, or remove any stone or layer you made, in the app.
  • Block someone, so you no longer see each other's stones and layers.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by writing to leystones@protonmail.me. We answer within 30 days.

If you live in Canada, you have rights under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights to access, correct, erase, restrict and move your data, and to complain to your local authority. We rely on your consent for location, camera, photos and microphone, and on providing the service you asked for in the rest.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase in Canada (Montreal). If that is outside your country, the laws there may differ from yours.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Each person can read only what they are allowed to, enforced in the database itself. No system is perfectly safe; if a breach puts you at real risk, we will tell you.

Children

Leystones is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
